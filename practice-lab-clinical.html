In design
Simulated patients only. Every person in this lab is fictional and generated for training. Do not type real patient information here. This lab runs on the non-clinical Practice Lab path, not the BAA-covered clinical path used by the Scribe and the other clinical tools. It is training, not clinical advice, and nothing here is a substitute for supervision.
