Ending care is a clinical and legal workflow, not just a letter. This tool walks you through reason, clinical appropriateness, abandonment-risk safeguards, notice, continuity planning, and documentation, then generates the patient letter, chart note, an admin checklist, and an optional scheduling alert. AI-assisted, you review and own the output, nothing is stored.
