This page reflects the current operational status of the Think Beyond Practice clinical platform. Clinical processing runs on AWS (Lambda and Amazon Bedrock) in US regions and transcription on Microsoft Azure AI Speech, under Business Associate Agreements. Planned maintenance is announced in advance and scheduled in low-use windows; in-progress notes and recordings are preserved on the clinician's device and can be recovered after any interruption.
