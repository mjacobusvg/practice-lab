The community

Reason through real cases with peers who prescribe.

Not a Facebook group, not a comment section. A structured peer forum where practicing psychiatric prescribers work through billing edge cases, documentation strategy, diagnostic complexity, case discussions, and practice-management questions. The thread history alone is worth the membership.

What gets worked through here, by topic:

Billing & coding

Which E/M level the note actually supports, denials and ERAs, modifiers, and the calls that keep getting clawed back.

"Is this really a 99215, or still a 99214?"

Documentation & audit

What actually holds up when a note is audited, MDM versus time, and writing an assessment you can defend.

"What would have held up this 99214 in an audit?"

Diagnostic complexity

Differential, comorbidity, and the evaluations where the picture does not fit the checklist.

"ADHD, bipolar II, or both, and how you told them apart."

Medication decisions

Interactions, cross-titrations, augmentation, and the tougher prescribing calls.

"Cross-titrating off venlafaxine on a sensitive patient. How did you pace it?"

Case discussions

Bring a de-identified case and think it through with people who prescribe.

"New eval, everything points three directions. Help me think."

Running a practice

Credentialing, rate setting, no-shows, and the logistics of a solo practice.

"What do you charge for a 90837, and how did you land there?"
