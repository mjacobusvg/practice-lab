Paste a clinical note. The tool removes protected health information in two passes: an AI pass that strips names, employers, schools, and place names, then a deterministic pass that guarantees dates, IDs, phone numbers, and other shaped identifiers are gone. Returns a de-identified note you can use for case discussion or outside reasoning.

This runs through the platform's protected pathway. Your note is processed under the practice's BAA and is not stored. Even so, read the output before you use it anywhere, and de-identify one note at a time.
