Clinical Note Builder

Generate psychotherapy and assessment documentation from your HPI. Paste the HPI you already wrote or dictated, and this builds the two sections that are the most cognitively demanding and the most audit-sensitive: a code-ready psychotherapy add-on blurb and a unified clinical assessment formulation. It does not generate the HPI or the Plan. Those stay yours.

Documentation aid, not a clinical or coding authority. Output is generated from the text you provide and must be reviewed, corrected, and signed by you. This tool writes the sections; it does not assign or guarantee a billing code. Verify medical necessity, time, and code selection yourself, and run the finished note through the Chart Audit + Coder to confirm what it supports. No patient data is stored.
PHI is processed transiently to generate your sections and is not retained.
