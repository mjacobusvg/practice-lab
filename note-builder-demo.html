Clinical Note Builder

⚠Do not enter PHI or personal health information. This tool is not covered by a Business Associate Agreement and is not intended for protected health information. Do not enter any information that identifies a patient, and do not enter health information about yourself or anyone else. Keep all entries free of names, contact details, and other identifying or health-related specifics.
Generate psychotherapy and assessment documentation from your HPI. It builds the two sections that are the most cognitively demanding and the most audit-sensitive — a code-ready psychotherapy add-on blurb and a unified clinical assessment formulation. It does not generate the HPI or the Plan. Those stay yours.
Documentation aid, not a clinical or coding authority. Output is generated from the text you provide and must be reviewed, corrected, and signed by you. This tool writes the sections; it does not assign or guarantee a billing code. Run the finished note through the Chart Audit + Coder to confirm what it supports. No patient data is stored.
