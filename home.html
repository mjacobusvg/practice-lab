Think Beyond AI Live

Your AI scribe and clinical assistant.

Think beyond the scribe.

Built to support the way psychiatric clinicians actually think and work. Think Beyond AI does the clerical work around the visit without taking the clinical thinking away from you. It gets you ready before you walk in, keeps the unfinished threads from last visit in view, reads the outside records, drafts in your own template around the calls you made, and helps you reason through what you are seeing. Then it reads the finished note back like an auditor and shows you the E/M level your documentation can actually defend. The goal is not just to make you faster. It is to make the work easier while helping you get a little sharper every visit. You sign knowing it holds.

How it actually works

Before Get ready Prep takes the intake forms, prior notes, testing and collateral you already have and gets today's evaluation ready: a rundown to read before you walk in, and your own template filled with the relevant history. Review Outside Records reads a prior evaluation or an old packet on its own. Scans and faxes read too.
→
During Work the visit One tab per patient, all day. Scribe drafts an HPI in your template, a reasoning-first assessment and a code-ready psychotherapy add-on from the calls you made. Discern thinks through the case beside your note. Frameworks organise the evidence when a diagnostic question is the hard part.
→
After Audit & code Audit + Coder reads your finished note the way a chart auditor would: internal contradictions, thin rationale, medication and problem reconciliation gaps. Then the E/M level your documentation actually supports, and whether the add-on is defensible, with the reasoning behind every call.

Screeners, a therapy guide, and one-tap links to your other tools live right in the working window.

Yes, it asks a little of you up front. That is the point. Because you make the clinical calls instead of repairing a black box's guess, you rarely touch the final note. It is not built to make you think less. It is built to make you a little sharper every visit.

Next

  • Structured Interviews, ADHD first. Starts from what the records already establish and works through what still needs assessing, instead of marching you through a static checklist you have half-answered already.

On the roadmap

  • Medication Intelligence. Recognise the medications already in the case and surface clinically meaningful interactions, explain why they matter, identify what raises or lowers concern, and support monitoring and documentation without making you re-enter the medication list.
  • Monitoring Support. Use the medications, diagnoses and history already available to surface labs, vitals, ECGs and other monitoring worth considering, show what is missing, and carry the monitoring plan you choose into the note.
  • Context-Aware Letters & Forms. FMLA and PFML, accommodations, disability, return to work, medical necessity, and more. Know what the document still requires while the patient is in front of you, then use what was established during the visit to build it.
  • Longitudinal Case View. Turn the notes and records you provide into a usable timeline of medication trials, symptoms, labs, diagnoses, treatment response and major clinical decisions, so you can see what changed instead of rereading the chart.
  • Connected Clinical Tools. When the visit calls for an interaction review, a safety plan, a letter, a form or a chart audit, carry the context already in the workspace into that tool instead of making you enter everything again.

Exploring

  • EHR-connected context. Where integrations allow it, bring medications, diagnoses, labs, appointments and prior notes into the workspace directly instead of requiring copy and paste or uploads. This one depends on outside vendors and their APIs, so it is honestly labelled: we are exploring it, not promising it.

Practice Lab

You make mistakes here first — not on real claims

Guided trainings and simulation-based practice built around the decisions that actually cost you money, time, and sleep. Every scenario uses real chart notes, real ERA responses, and real consequences. Get stuck, and you can call an AI insurance rep the same way you would on a real call.

  • Guided Billing Training — the full billing cycle from practice setup through patient billing. NPI, fee schedule, credentialing, eligibility verification, claim building, ERA interpretation, and payment posting. Seven modules, explained as you do it.
  • Note Building & MDM Training — build a defensible psychotherapy note step by step. Three units covering psychotherapy documentation, MDM vs time-based billing, and how your documentation proves the level you billed to an auditor.
  • Billing Simulator — submit a claim, get a real outcome, then figure out what to do about it
  • Denial Drills — 16 scenarios that replicate the exact moment after a claim comes back wrong
  • MDM Foundations — work through chart notes and decide whether the documentation proves the level you billed
  • Psychotherapy Documentation — practice writing defensible 90833/90836/90837 notes under realistic conditions
  • More modes shipping: Chart Coder, Paper Remittance, Sandbox

Practice Manager

The clinical and operational toolkit your EHR should have but doesn't

Tools for the work that pulls you away from patient care. Clinical tools you can pull up mid-session and operational infrastructure that runs your back office.

  • Assessment suite — send PHQ-9, GAD-7, PCL-5, AUDIT, MDQ and more via tokenized link, auto-scored, chart-note-ready
  • CYP450 interaction checker — paste a med list, get clinically relevant interactions with the mechanism and what to do
  • Letter generator — medical necessity appeals, ESA, FMLA, disability accommodation, work and school letters, and more. Enter details, select type, download ready to send
  • Crisis safety plan generator — Stanley-Brown format, customized to the patient, with chart note blurb ready to paste into your EHR
  • LAI administration guide — dosing, conversions, injection technique, patient counseling for every available LAI
  • Difficult conversation reference — scripts for declining controlled substance requests, nonadherence, hard news, ending treatment. Includes chart note blurb for each
  • Template hub — clinical, billing, compliance, and patient-facing templates. Homework generator, treatment plan generator, monitoring protocols, augmentation reference
  • HIPAA compliance binder — answer guided questions, auto-generate compliance docs, stored when you need them. Cross-state reference, payer contract manager, superbill generator, termination letters with chart note blurb, incident documentation, CE and supervision tracking
