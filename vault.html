The Vault

Your complete practice profile. Enter your information once; every tool across Think Beyond Practice reads from here.

Your data is stored securely and used only to pre-fill your tools. No information is shared with third parties. You can export or delete your data at any time.
⚠️
Important: Back up your Vault to secure cloud storage

Until recently, Vault data was only saved on the device where you originally entered it due to a backend issue. That issue is now fixed, but your data still needs a one-time push to cloud storage to sync across devices.

If your Vault information is showing below:

Scroll down, review your information, and click Save All at the bottom of any tab. You only need to do this once.

If your Vault is empty or missing information:

Your data is likely still saved on a different device. Do not start re-entering data here yet. Instead:

  1. Go back to the device where you originally filled out your Vault
  2. Open the Vault on that device and click Save All
  3. Return to this device and reload the page
  4. Your data should now appear here

If you cannot access the original device, contact support before entering new data so we can help recover or merge your information.
