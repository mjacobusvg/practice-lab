Think Beyond AI Live

An AI scribe, and more than a scribe.

Think beyond the scribe.

Built to support the way psychiatric clinicians actually think and work. It does the clerical work around the visit, before, during and after, without ever making the clinical call for you. The goal is not only to make you faster. It is to make the work easier while you get a little sharper every visit.

Before
Get ready

Prep turns intake, prior notes, testing and collateral into a rundown to read before you walk in, and fills your own template with the relevant history. Review Outside Records reads a prior evaluation or an old packet on its own, scans and faxes included.

During
Work the visit

One tab per patient, all day. Scribe drafts an HPI in your template and a reasoning-first assessment from the calls you made. The therapy guide helps you name the technique you were already using and put it into defensible documentation. Discern thinks through the case beside your note.

After
Audit & code

Audit + Coder reads your finished note for contradictions, thin rationale and reconciliation gaps, then shows the E/M level your documentation actually supports, with the reasoning behind every call. Knowing coding academically and doing it confidently in real practice are two different things. It teaches as it checks.

Screeners, a therapy guide, and one-tap links to your other tools live right in the working window. Structured Interviews, beginning with ADHD, are next.
