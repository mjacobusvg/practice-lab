When do I leave the usual toolbox?

Each of these earns its place by solving something your familiar option does not.

The problemConsiderWhy it exists
Adderall XR works, but consistently does not last long enoughMydayisThe extended-coverage branch of mixed amphetamine. In development, 37.5 mg produced exposure like XR 25 mg plus IR 12.5 mg eight hours later. Built to replace that second dose.

Four things I will forget

  1. A 37.5 mg amphetamine/dextroamphetamine ER in the EHR is a Mydayis-type product, not Adderall XR. Adderall XR runs 5–30 mg; Mydayis runs 12.5, 25, 37.5, 50. The label says do not substitute one for the other milligram-for-milligram.
  2. Adderall XR works but needs a booster every single day? That is the Mydayis indication, stated plainly.
  3. Vyvanse works but the morning is the problem? Dyanavel is the relevant one. Mydayis is not shown to start faster.
  4. Nothing taken after waking can start early enough? Jornay PM. That is a different problem from "does not last long enough."

Coverage reality

Insurance varies by plan, but this is why many of us end up prescribing from the same small toolbox.

Usually the easier path
  • generic Adderall IR / XR
  • generic lisdexamfetamine
  • generic methylphenidate IR / ER
  • generic dexmethylphenidate
More obtainable than the name suggests
  • Mydayis-type ER amphetamine — generic available
  • Adzenys-type ER amphetamine ODT — generic available
  • Daytrana-type MPH patch — generic available
Expect friction — check first
  • Jornay PM
  • Azstarys
  • Dyanavel XR
  • Xelstrym
  • QuilliChew ER
  • Quillivant / Cotempla — generic approval and market availability can be messy

Generic availability does not guarantee coverage. Brand status does not guarantee denial. Check the patient's actual plan. Status shifts — reviewed September 2026.
