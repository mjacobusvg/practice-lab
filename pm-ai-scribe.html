Why we ask for both
Your blank template gives the tool your complete structure — every section and symptom menu you use, across all patients. Your completed HPI teaches it your voice and level of detail — how you actually write. One completed note only covers the one patient's issues, so on its own it can miss whole sections you use; the scaffold fills those in. Together they build a template that has all your sections and sounds like you.
