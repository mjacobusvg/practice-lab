Turn your visit into a clean clinical note in your structure and voice. Type your notes as you go, prep from last visit, or record the visit (in person or telehealth) — the AI Scribe reorganizes exactly what you documented into your note, using only what you wrote. Documentation aid, not a clinical authority. The HPI is generated only from the notes you provide and must be reviewed, corrected, and signed by you. It never adds symptoms, denials, history, or findings you did not document. No patient data is retained on our servers: notes are processed transiently, and any recording is uploaded only for transcription and deleted afterward. A working draft is saved on your own device so a crash or accidental close can be recovered, and it is cleared when you finish the patient.

Why we ask for both
Your blank template gives the tool your complete structure — every section and symptom menu you use, across all patients. Your completed HPI teaches it your voice and level of detail — how you actually write. One completed note only covers the one patient's issues, so on its own it can miss whole sections you use; the scaffold fills those in. Together they build a template that has all your sections and sounds like you.
